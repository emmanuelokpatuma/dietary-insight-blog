Common questions

What is a healthy BMI in the UK?

For most adults the NHS healthy range is 18.5 to 24.9. Overweight is 25 to 29.9 and obese is 30 or more.

Are the BMI cut-offs different for some ethnic groups?

Yes. NICE advises lower thresholds for people of Black African, African-Caribbean, South Asian, Chinese and Middle Eastern background: overweight from 23 and obese from 27.5.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It can overestimate for muscular people and underestimate for some older adults. It is a screening guide, not a diagnosis, and it is not for children or pregnancy.

Does weight affect blood sugar and blood pressure?

Carrying extra weight raises the risk of type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure, and even modest weight loss can help. Ask your GP for support that suits you.
