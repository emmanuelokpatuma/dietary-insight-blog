The Dietary Insight Blog publishes a short, practical guide every day on eating well with diabetes, high blood pressure and food allergies.

## Why we write it

Emmanuel founded SimplerLivez and built the [Dietary Insight](/) app because he lives with type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure. Deciding at every meal whether a food is right for you is tiring, especially when there is no label. This blog shares what we learn along the way, in plain English.

## How we keep it accurate

- Every article is checked against UK sources such as the NHS, Diabetes UK, the British Heart Foundation and the Food Standards Agency, and links to them.
- We never tell you to change your medication, and we don't claim that any food or app treats or cures a condition.
- Articles are reviewed by a person before they are published, and updated when guidance changes.

Spotted something wrong? Tell us through [simplerlivez.com](https://simplerlivez.com) and we'll fix it.

## About the app

Dietary Insight lets you take a photo of a meal and see the nutrients that matter to you, any allergens from your profile, and a healthier swap. It's free on Google Play and the App Store, with 7 days of Premium for every new user.
