## community_answer
Where: r/diabetes_t2, or Quora: "What are the early warning signs of type 2 diabetes?"

The NHS lists the most common symptoms as feeling very tired, peeing more than usual, feeling thirsty all the time and losing weight without trying. Other signs include blurred vision, cuts taking longer to heal, and genital itching or repeated thrush. Diabetes UK sums up the main ones as the 4 Ts: Toilet, Thirsty, Tired, Thinner.

The catch is that not everyone gets symptoms. Diabetes UK says type 2 can go undiagnosed for years because it develops slowly, so your risk matters too. The NHS says risk is higher if you're white and over 40, or over 25 and from a South Asian, Chinese, Black African or Black Caribbean background, if a parent or sibling has it, if you're overweight and not very active, or if you have high blood pressure.

Diabetes UK's free Know Your Risk tool takes about two minutes. If you're worried, ask your GP for a blood test. If symptoms come on quickly or you feel very unwell, Diabetes UK says to get an urgent GP appointment or call 111. The NHS says to call 999 if you have signs like being sick, fast breathing, fruity-smelling breath or confusion.
