Where: r/diabetes_t2, or Quora: "Can people with diabetes eat jollof rice?"

Yes, jollof rice can still fit into a healthy diet with diabetes. Diabetes UK even publishes its own jollof rice and chicken recipe. The main things to look at are the type of rice, the portion, and what goes into the pot.

Rice: Diabetes UK suggests brown rice or basmati instead of white rice, and its recipe uses brown basmati. NHS Healthier Families says easy-cook brown rice works in jollof but takes an extra 10 to 15 minutes.

Portion: Diabetes UK says the amount of carbs has a bigger effect on blood sugar than GI alone. Serving jollof with grilled chicken or fish and steamed veg or a salad, as NHS Healthier Families suggests, means rice doesn't have to fill the plate.

Salt and oil: use a reduced-salt stock cube and measure the oil. The Diabetes UK recipe uses 2 tsp of rapeseed oil for four servings. The NHS says adults should have no more than 6g of salt a day.

For reference, each 393g serving of the Diabetes UK recipe has 377kcal and 50.3g of carbohydrate.

Leftovers: the NHS says rice shouldn't be reheated more than once.
