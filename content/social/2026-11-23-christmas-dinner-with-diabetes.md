Where: r/diabetes_t2, or Quora: "What can I eat for Christmas dinner if I have diabetes?"

You can still enjoy Christmas dinner. Diabetes UK says a short break from your usual routine is normal, and one or two high readings shouldn't affect your long-term health or diabetes management, as long as you don't let your blood sugar stay high for long and get back to your usual habits afterwards.

On the plate, the British Heart Foundation says turkey is a lean meat as long as it isn't basted in too much butter. Go easy on roast potatoes and parsnips, and note that pigs in blankets, stuffing and gravy are usually high in salt. Diabetes UK suggests filling your plate with vegetables first, serving yourself so you control portions, and watching for hidden sugars in cranberry sauce and other condiments. For dessert, stick to one portion and try natural yoghurt instead of double cream.

If you drink, the guideline is no more than 14 units a week. Diabetes UK says not to drink on an empty stomach if you use insulin or some other diabetes medications, because it raises the risk of a hypo, and that risk can last up to 24 hours.

Before the holidays, stock up on medicines, and remember NHS 111 for urgent advice while your GP surgery is closed.
