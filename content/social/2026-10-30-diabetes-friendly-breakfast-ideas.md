Where: r/diabetes_t2, or Quora: "What is a good breakfast for type 2 diabetes?"

A good breakfast for type 2 diabetes mixes slow-release carbs, some fibre and a bit of protein, which tends to keep you fuller and avoids a big sugar spike.

Easy UK supermarket options:
- Porridge made with plain oats, topped with berries
- Plain (unsweetened) yoghurt with fruit and a sprinkle of seeds or nuts
- Eggs (boiled, poached or scrambled) on wholegrain toast
- Wholegrain toast with peanut butter (no added sugar)
- Leftover veg in an omelette

Things to watch:
- Many breakfast cereals and granolas are high in sugar even when they look healthy. Check for more than 22.5g sugar per 100g (that's high).
- Fruit juice and smoothies count as free sugars, so whole fruit is a better choice.
- Flavoured yoghurts and pastries add up quickly.

Portions still matter even with "good" foods, and if you're on medication that can cause hypos, ask your diabetes nurse before changing when or how much you eat.
