Where: r/diabetes_t2, or Quora: "What are the best snacks for type 2 diabetes?"

First, you don't always need to snack. Plenty of people with type 2 manage fine on three meals. But if you do get hungry between meals, simple everyday foods work best:

- 10 almonds: about 61 calories
- 2 satsumas: about 44 calories
- A kiwi: about 25 calories
- Veg sticks (carrot, cucumber, pepper) with a little hummus
- Plain yoghurt
- An oatcake or two
- Unsalted nuts (a small handful, they add up)

Easy swaps:
- Crisps → plain popcorn or unsalted nuts
- Chocolate → a couple of squares of dark chocolate, or fruit
- Sweets → fresh or frozen berries

If you're snacking when you're not actually hungry, it can help to drink some water first, keep snacks out of sight and notice what triggers it (boredom, stress, the TV).
