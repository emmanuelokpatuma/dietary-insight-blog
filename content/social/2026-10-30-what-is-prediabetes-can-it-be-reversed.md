Where: r/prediabetes, or Quora: "Can prediabetes be reversed?"

Prediabetes means your blood sugar is higher than normal but not in the type 2 diabetes range yet. In the UK it's usually diagnosed with an HbA1c test:

- Below 42 mmol/mol: normal
- 42 to 47 mmol/mol: prediabetes
- 48 or above: type 2 diabetes range

Diabetes UK says it's possible to bring your numbers back into the normal range with health changes, and it doesn't go away on its own without them. The changes that help are the everyday ones: moving more, eating more fibre, veg and wholegrains, cutting back on sugary drinks and processed food, and losing weight if you're carrying extra, particularly around the middle. Sleep and stopping smoking matter too.

Weight isn't the whole story. Age, family history and ethnicity also play a part (people of Black African, African Caribbean and South Asian backgrounds are at higher risk).

In England you may be offered the free NHS Healthier You programme, which is designed exactly for this. Ask your GP, and ask how often to re-check your HbA1c.
