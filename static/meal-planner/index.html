Common questions

Where do the recipes come from?

Every dinner is a published recipe from Diabetes UK or NHS Healthier Families. Each card links to the original recipe, and the calories, carbs and salt shown are the per-serving figures from that recipe page.

Is this meal plan right for my diabetes?

It is general healthy-eating inspiration, not a personal plan. How much carbohydrate is right for you depends on you and any medication you take, so talk to your GP, diabetes nurse or dietitian about your own needs.

Does the planner check for allergens?

No. If you have a food allergy, read the full recipe and check the label of every ingredient you buy, every time.
