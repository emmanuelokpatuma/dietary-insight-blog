Common questions

How do I convert sodium to salt?

Multiply sodium by 2.5 to get salt. For example 1 g sodium is 2.5 g salt. To go the other way, multiply salt by 0.4.

How much salt should I have a day in the UK?

Adults should have no more than 6 g of salt a day, which is about 2.4 g of sodium. Children need less, depending on age.

Why do some labels show sodium instead of salt?

Some labels, especially from other countries, list sodium only. Salt is the sodium plus chloride, so the salt figure is about 2.5 times higher.

Does salt affect blood pressure?

Yes. Eating too much salt can raise blood pressure over time. Cutting back, and choosing lower-salt options, can help.
