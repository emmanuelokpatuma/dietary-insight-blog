Common questions

How many calories are in jollof rice?

A typical home-style jollof rice has roughly 150 to 170 kcal per 100 g cooked, so a 250 g plate is around 400 kcal before any meat, fish or plantain. The oil used is the biggest variable.

Why do the calories vary so much?

African dishes are cooked to family recipes. The amount of palm or vegetable oil, the cuts of meat, and the size of the plate all change the total, which is why this calculator lets you adjust grams and oil.

Are these numbers exact?

No. They are estimates based on typical recipes and published food data, roughly plus or minus 15%. For a precise figure for your own recipe, you need to weigh the ingredients.

Can I count calories in African food with an app?

Yes. Dietary Insight lets you take a photo of your meal and get an estimate of calories, sugar, salt and cholesterol, which is handy for dishes that are missing from most calorie counters.
