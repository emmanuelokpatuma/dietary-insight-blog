Common questions

How much sugar should I have per day in the UK?

UK guidance says free sugars should make up no more than 5% of your daily energy. In practice that is about 30 g a day for adults and children aged 11 and over, 24 g for ages 7 to 10, and 19 g for ages 4 to 6.

What counts as free sugars?

Free sugars are sugars added to foods and drinks, plus sugars naturally in honey, syrups and fruit juices. Sugars in whole fruit, vegetables and plain milk are not counted.

How many teaspoons is 30 g of sugar?

Roughly 7 teaspoons, counting one level teaspoon as about 4 g.

I have diabetes. Is this limit right for me?

Not necessarily. This is general guidance for the public. People with diabetes or prediabetes usually get personal targets from their care team, often focused on total carbohydrate as well as sugar, so follow their advice.
