Common questions

How do I convert HbA1c % to mmol/mol?

Subtract 2.15 from the percentage and multiply by 10.929. For example 6.5% is about 48 mmol/mol.

What is a normal HbA1c in the UK?

Below 42 mmol/mol (6.0%) is normal. 42 to 47 mmol/mol is the prediabetes range and 48 mmol/mol (6.5%) or above is in the diabetes range.

What is estimated average glucose?

It is the average blood glucose that a given HbA1c roughly corresponds to over the past 2 to 3 months. It is an estimate and can differ from your meter readings.

Can I lower my HbA1c?

Many people do through food choices, activity, weight loss where advised, and medicines where prescribed. Ask your care team for a plan that suits you.
