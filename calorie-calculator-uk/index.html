Common questions

How many calories should I eat a day in the UK?

NHS guidance gives about 2,500 kcal a day for men and 2,000 kcal for women as a rough average, but your own figure depends on your age, size and activity. This calculator estimates yours.

What is TDEE?

Total daily energy expenditure is the number of calories you burn in a day, including your resting energy (BMR) and everything you do. Eating around that number keeps your weight steady.

How is the calculation done?

It uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate your resting energy, then multiplies by an activity factor. A deficit of about 500 kcal a day is roughly 0.5 kg of weight loss a week.

Why won't it show a very low calorie target?

Eating below about 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) a day is generally only advised under medical supervision, so the target is raised to that floor.
