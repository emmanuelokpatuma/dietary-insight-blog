Common questions

How do I convert mmol/L to mg/dL?

Multiply mmol/L by 18. For example 7 mmol/L is about 126 mg/dL. To go the other way, divide mg/dL by 18.

Why do UK and US meters show different units?

The UK and most of Europe use mmol/L, while the US and some other countries use mg/dL. The reading is the same, only the unit differs.

What is a normal blood sugar level in the UK?

For people with diabetes the NHS suggests 4 to 7 mmol/L before meals and under 8.5 mmol/L (type 2) or 9 mmol/L (type 1) two hours after. Your care team may set personal targets.

When should I get help for a high or low reading?

Follow the plan from your diabetes team. Seek urgent help if you feel very unwell, confused, are vomiting, or have a very low reading that does not recover after treating it.
