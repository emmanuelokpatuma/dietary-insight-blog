Easy ways to hit it

  • 1 egg: about 6 g
  • 150 g Greek yoghurt: about 15 g
  • 100 g cooked chicken breast: about 30 g
  • 100 g tinned tuna or cooked fish fillet: about 20-25 g
  • 150 g cooked beans or lentils: about 12 g
  • 100 g tofu: about 12 g

Food values are typical averages and vary by brand and cooking method.

On a weight-loss jab? Appetite often drops, so small protein-rich meals can help. Follow the advice of your prescriber or dietitian.
This is an estimate for healthy adults, not medical advice. If you have kidney disease, are pregnant, or have a medical condition, check your protein target with your GP or dietitian first.

How this calculator works

It multiplies your body weight by a daily protein factor for your goal: 0.8 g/kg for general health (in line with the UK reference intake of 0.75 g/kg), around 1.2 g/kg for active people and adults over 65, 1.4 g/kg when losing weight, and 1.6 g/kg for muscle building. The range shown is about 10% either side.
